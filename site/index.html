How to use Yomeru

Kanji courses teach each character with a handful of example words. Once you know a few dozen kanji, there are hundreds of everyday words you can already read that nobody has shown you. Yomeru finds them in about 31,000 words from JMdict, the open Japanese–English dictionary.

  1. Pick where your kanji come fromUse the tabs at the top: WaniKani, a JLPT level, a school grade, or my list (paste any kanji or Japanese text).
  2. Read your wordsThe most common words come first, grouped by the level that unlocked them. Search, or filter by word type.
  3. Tap a wordSee every meaning, the kanji it uses and a link to Jisho. Mark words you already know to hide them.
  4. Take them with youExport makes an Anki deck, or text to paste into other flashcard apps.
Using WaniKani? How to get your token (1 minute)
  1. Open WaniKani › Settings › API Tokens and sign in if asked.
  2. Press Generate a new token. Give it any name, like "Yomeru", and leave the boxes unticked: read-only is all Yomeru needs.
  3. Copy the token, open the WaniKani tab here and paste it in.

A read-only token can see your progress but can't change anything. It goes straight from your browser to WaniKani and nowhere else, and you can delete it on WaniKani at any time.
